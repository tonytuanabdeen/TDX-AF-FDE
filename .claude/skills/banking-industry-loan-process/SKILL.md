# Banking Industry Loan Process

Manages the complete loan processing lifecycle from initial application through final disbursement. Each stage enforces validation rules, eligibility checks, and compliance gates before advancing to the next step.

## Process Stages

### 1. Application Intake
- Collect borrower information and loan request details
- Validate required fields and data formats
- Perform initial eligibility screening against product criteria
- Generate a unique loan application reference

### 2. Document Collection and Verification
- Request supporting documents (income verification, identification, collateral records)
- Validate document authenticity and completeness
- Flag missing or expired documents before proceeding
- Maintain a secure document audit trail

### 3. Credit Evaluation
- Pull credit reports from authorized bureaus
- Calculate debt-to-income ratios and credit risk scores
- Apply institution-specific credit policy thresholds
- Document all credit decision factors for regulatory review

### 4. Underwriting Decision
- Evaluate application against underwriting guidelines
- Apply risk-based pricing adjustments
- Generate approval, conditional approval, or denial with documented rationale
- Route exceptions to senior underwriters when policy limits are exceeded

### 5. Loan Disbursement
- Confirm all pre-disbursement conditions are satisfied
- Generate loan agreements and disclosure documents
- Execute fund transfer upon borrower acceptance
- Record disbursement details for servicing and reporting

## Compliance and Governance
- All decisions must include documented rationale for auditability
- Personally identifiable information must be handled per data protection regulations
- Anti-money laundering and know-your-customer checks must complete before disbursement
- Process deviations require documented exception approvals
