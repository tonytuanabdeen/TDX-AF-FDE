Person Account Model
Important: Person Accounts must be enabled at the org level. Once enabled, this setting cannot be reversed.

Person Accounts combine Account and Contact into a single record for individuals (B2C). Each loan applicant is a Person Account — no separate Contact record is needed.

Account → Loan_Application__c: The Applicant__c lookup targets the Account object. When Person Accounts are enabled, this resolves to Person Account records (IsPersonAccount = true). No separate Contact lookup is needed.
Loan_Application__c → Loan_QA__c: Master-Detail ensures Q&A records inherit sharing from the parent and are cascade-deleted when the parent is removed.
Design Decisions
DecisionRationale
Person Account over Account + Contact Banking customers are individuals, not businesses. Person Accounts provide a single record per customer, simplifying data access and eliminating Account-Contact joins.
Single Applicant lookup (no Contact__c) With Person Accounts, contact-level fields (email, phone, address) are available via Person* prefixed fields directly on the Account. A separate Contact lookup is redundant.
Master-Detail for Loan Q&A Q&A records have no meaning outside their parent Loan Application. Master-Detail enforces this lifecycle dependency and enables roll-up summaries.
Restrict delete on Applicant Prevents accidental deletion of a Person Account with active loan applications, preserving data integrity.
Flexible Q&A model Generic Question/Answer child object avoids schema changes when new qualification questions are added to the loan process.
