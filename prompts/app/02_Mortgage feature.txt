### 3. Questions (these map to what the Agentforce agent asks in `sfdc/prompts/02_Agent.txt`)

The wizard collects the same data the agent collects — in the same order — so the answers can be pre-filled when the user switches to chat:

1. What is your current work situation? (picklist: Employed Full-Time, Employed Part-Time, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Student)
2. What is your annual net income (after tax)? (currency)
3. What is the total purchase price of the property? (currency)
4. How much would you like to borrow? (currency)
5. Over how many months would you like to repay the loan? (text/number)
